# Demo walkthrough (about 3 minutes)

## 0:00–0:25 — Story page: hook

“Electricity powers the whole country, but it isn’t produced evenly. This story
asks where France’s regions generate more or less electricity than they use,
and what the exchange data can—and cannot—tell us.”

Point out the framing, audience, research question, and chapter navigation. Mention
that the shared year range and region focus are controlled from the sidebar.

## 0:25–1:10 — National overview: the regional balance

Use the map and ranked exchange chart. Explain the sign: positive physical
exchange is import; negative is export. Hover over two contrasting regions.
Emphasize that these are regional balances, not direct links between regions.

## 1:10–1:40 — Production coverage and trend

Show the 100% reference line and compare regions above and below it. Explain
that this is production divided by consumption—not a claim of energy
independence. Change the monthly trend selector and mention transmission,
storage, and losses.

## 1:40–2:15 — Regional explorer

Point to monthly generation bands and dotted demand line. Change the focus
region, then show the renewable-share heatmap or compare a few regions.

## 2:15–2:45 — What-if lab

Increase the wind-and-solar slider. Describe the assumptions shown next to the
result: fixed demand, fixed other generation, and extra output reducing imports
before increasing exports. Stress that it is not a grid dispatch forecast.

## 2:45–3:00 — Takeaways and data

Close with the implications and open the data notes. Show the filtered-data
download and remind viewers that the caveats and source attribution are included.
